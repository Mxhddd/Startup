Safety system

Before: CNIC, licence and reference checks; training in driving, first aid, child safety and breakdowns. During: fixed routes; every tap visible to the captain; "I'm stuck" alerts captain and parents; emergency sends location to the captain; 15 and 1122 one tap away. After: weekly reliability report to the captain; no automatic penalties.

12-week pilot

Three Baji vans in one Karachi neighbourhood: one woman driver, one mother-driver, one male driver with a woman attendant. We measure: driver tap rate ≥ 80%, driver income against target, families still subscribed at week 12, and zero safety incidents.
