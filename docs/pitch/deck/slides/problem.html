7:02 · the van is late

Every school morning, ten families call one driver.

Families pay Rs 2,000–7,500 a child each month for a private van, and get no news from it. Most vans are one owner-driver: no office, no tracker, no record. The driver answers the calls at the wheel.

“وین نکل گئی؟” has the van left?

“علی بیٹھ گیا؟” is Ali in the van?

“کتنی دیر؟” how long now?

Van fees: bestschool.pk 2025, housingassociates.pk 2026. Calls per day: to be counted in the pilot van.

Make it personal: ask the room how many of them called a van driver this week.
