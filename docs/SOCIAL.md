## 2. What went wrong

1. **Reels are the whole game, and we posted one.** One Reel brought more reach (886) than all ten image
   posts together (844), and 12 of the 14 follows. Instagram shows Reels to people who don't follow you;
   it shows image posts mostly to the 51 who do.
2. **Seven posts in one hour on 17 Nov.** They competed with each other for the same 51 followers, and
   each later post reached fewer people (41, 37…). After that, one post a day of the same kind faded: 39,
   47, 25.
3. **Ads, not stories.** Most posts explained the app. Nobody shares an ad. The Van Uncle meme was the
   best image post (185 reach) because people recognised themselves in it.
4. **TikTok is not being shown at all** (2 views, 0 profile views). A brand-new account posting from a
   computer, with no warm-up, gets almost no reach. TikTok has to be posted from a phone, with a sound from
   TikTok's own library.
5. **The Facebook Page and LinkedIn company page have nobody to show posts to.** Pages with 0 to 1
   followers get no reach. On both, the reach lives on personal profiles (your LinkedIn video got 412
   impressions; the company page got 1) and, on Facebook, in parent groups.
6. **Nothing asked people to do anything small.** 0 saves, 2 comments. Posts didn't ask for a comment,
   a tag or a save, which are the signals that make Instagram show a post to more people.

## 3. The new rules

1. **One Reel a day, every day, at about 9 PM PKT** (after kids are asleep, the parents' scrolling hour).
   Test 7:30 AM on two days, when parents live the problem.
2. **Relatable first, product last.** Every Reel starts with a moment every Karachi parent knows (the
   class group at 7:14, calling Van Uncle, "5 minute"), and only the last 4 seconds show Pohnch.
3. **The first second is a hook in big text.** The words on screen say why to keep watching.
4. **Ask for one small action** in every caption: comment a number, tag someone, save it.
5. **Reply to every comment within an hour** of posting; that keeps the post being shown.
6. **No more than 2 feed posts a day**, never in a burst.
7. **TikTok from the phone**, with a trending sound at low volume under our video, 1 a day.
8. **Facebook: post each Reel on the Page, then share it from your own profile**, and share the comment
   posts into Karachi parent groups whose rules allow it (you post these yourself, as a parent).
9. **LinkedIn from your personal profile** (building in public, real numbers), company page reshares.
10. **Use Instagram "Trial reels"** (in the Reel share screen) for the second post of a day: it is shown
    only to non-followers, which is what a 51-follower account needs.

Success for the next 14 days: one Reel above 5,000 views; 300 Instagram followers; 100 sign-ups on
pohnch.pk/app; 10 drivers in WhatsApp.

What the numbers say:
- **The new animated Reels reach 128–207 people each and bring 0 follows.** The 17 Nov launch Reel still leads
  (889 reach, 12 follows): it was the account's first post, longer (34 s) and had music people know.
- **People leave at 4–7 seconds of a 16–17 second Reel** (24–41% watched), before Pohnch appears. Fix: the
  answer on screen from the first frame and under 10 seconds. Fast cuts made: `gate_fast`, `calling_fast`,
  `dict_fast` (pohnch.pk/media/v2/).
- **Distribution, not content, is the gap.** Instagram bio link taps: 0 (the bio still says pohnch.pk, not
  pohnch.pk/links). Facebook Page: 0 followers, image posts 0 views. TikTok: nothing posted from the phone.
  WhatsApp Status and parent-group shares: not done.
- **LinkedIn personal is steady** (~400 impressions a week) and is the right channel for schools.
