## The short version

1. Build the 19 pilot screens (B1 to B9 in `BUILD_PROMPTS.md`).
2. Run one van for 10 school days with a free month.
3. Gate 1: tap rate 80%+ and 3 of the first 10 families pay Rs 199.
4. Register the company and payment accounts before taking money; move hosting to Vercel Pro.
5. Apply for free help and grants with the pilot numbers (NIC Karachi, your university's incubation center and the HEC Innovator Seed Fund, Hult Prize).
6. Grow to 30 paying vans, then consider a loan or investors.

Funding facts were found by web search on 29 October 2026; check each on the official page before applying.
